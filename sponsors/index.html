What is UIST?

The ACM Symposium on User Interface Software and Technology (UIST) is the leading forum for research in human-computer interaction (HCI) focusing on pioneering interactive technologies. UIST advances cutting-edge research in areas such as human-AI interaction, sensing technology, haptics, the metaverse, augmented and virtual reality (AR/VR), and computer-supported cooperative work.

The conference attracts 500–750 attendees, including industry leaders, academic faculty, and emerging researchers at all career stages (Ph.D., Master’s, and Bachelor’s).

When and Where is UIST 2026?

UIST 2026 will take place from November 2–5, 2026, at the GM Renaissance Center in Detroit, MI.

Why Sponsor UIST?

Sponsoring UIST unlocks unique opportunities for engagement, visibility, and community investment. Our sponsorship provides rich opportunities to:

  • 1. Engage with the UIST Community: Sponsors receive complimentary full-conference registrations, enabling your team to network and participate in all UIST events at no additional cost.
  • 2. Promote Your Organization: Sponsors gain visibility through several prominent avenues, including:
    • A dedicated sponsor session (with over 150 attendees last year).
    • A table in the main conference lobby for continuous interaction with attendees.
    • Your organization’s logo prominently displayed on the UIST website, virtual program, plenary slides, and in verbal acknowledgments throughout the conference.
  • 3. Support the HCI Community: Your sponsorship helps broaden participation and strengthen our community. Funds are used to offer lower registration rates for students, provide travel grants for those with limited funding, and host signature receptions and community-building events.
